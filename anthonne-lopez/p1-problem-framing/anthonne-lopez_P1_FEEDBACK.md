## Two fixes, in order

1. **Turn your question into a decision.** Field 1 currently asks "what's the total sales revenue of each branch each month?" — that is a *topic*. Ask instead: *who does what differently once the number arrives?* Here is your own canvas, rewritten one level up: *"Each month, before it begins, the Sales Manager sets each branch's sales target and decides where to run promotions. Predict: total sales revenue per branch for the coming month (unit: branch-month). If a branch's forecast falls below target, launch a promo or push sale products there **before** the month starts."* Same idea — yours — but now the prediction serves an action taken in advance.
2. **Put pesos on your errors.** Over-forecast a branch and you over-commit promo budget; under-forecast and you under-invest in a branch that could have sold more. Estimate each in pesos, then say which is worse. "95% accurate" tells the Sales Manager nothing they can act on; "an under-forecast costs us roughly ₱X in missed promo opportunity" does.
